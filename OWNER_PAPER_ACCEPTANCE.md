# Owner test checklist — verified Windows paper build

Start with the verified installer checkpoint in WINDOWS_ACCEPTANCE.md. This checklist collects owner-environment evidence; it is not a bank connection, broker-write onboarding or live-trading permission. Local testing uses virtual funds and needs no deposit or paid AI service.

1. **Prepare and install.** Preserve existing private Nexus data/backups before an upgrade. Record the installer version and Windows version. Install under your ordinary Windows account, launch Nexus and confirm the displayed application version. Record startup problems without publishing private paths or credentials.
2. **Review the ready-made setup.** Open To Do and Risk. On a fresh unused profile, expect the recommended virtual budget and Terrestrial allowance. Saved settings and explicit OFF choices should survive an upgrade; do not overwrite them to imitate a fresh install. The To Do checks should describe what is included, optional or still future work.
3. **Start the research engine.** Choose Start Portal. Confirm observation/research status updates. Built-in numerical models provide advisory research; a paid AI key is optional. Starting research should not independently engage paper trading.
4. **Inspect Matrix.** Singularity should show observed whole-account history; Fractal should show actual retained holding movement. A flat account or insufficient history may leave gaps or no position series. The app must not invent positions or substitute ticker movement for account returns.
5. **Engage local paper explicitly.** Review the virtual risk allowance and confirmation before Engage Paper. This runs the fixed BTC experiment, not an automatically promoted learned model or a funded broker account. Record useful status/error text and whether paper accounting agrees with displayed activity.
6. **Test the independent stop.** Use Emergency Stop during the local paper experiment. Verify execution is halted and pending simulated orders are cancelled while the research engine remains available. Retained positions may remain visible; a stop is not a promise to liquidate them. Reset must not engage trading automatically.
7. **Test restart.** Close and reopen Nexus. Check retained settings/account history and confirm that paper trading starts stopped. Do not treat stale session counters as permission to resume.
8. **Plan sustained observation.** After the build and supervised checks are stable, use To Do Paper Trial Readiness to plan a minimum 72-hour genuine public-feed paper window. Keep the computer/app running for local operation; computer-off cloud operation is not implemented. Retain outage/restart/emergency observations. Declared coverage, even at 95%, is a diagnostic input rather than complete strategy, broker or release acceptance.

## What to report

Provide the exact build version, Windows version, fresh-install versus upgrade status, which numbered step you tried, expected versus observed behavior and sanitized error text. Redact keys, account identifiers, balances and private paths from screenshots. Keep raw journals, response bodies and account exports private; do not attach them to public GitHub issues.

Coding can continue without owner setup. The separate connected-broker acceptance stage will require an authorized paper-only account, reviewed scoped credential handling and a supervised test plan. The internal paper POST and event-stream primitives are not yet a ready autonomous broker connection. No bank deposit or live account is needed for the local checklist above.
