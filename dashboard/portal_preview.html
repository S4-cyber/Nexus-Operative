Welcome // autonomous trading is the goal

Your autonomous trading engine

Nexus is being built to watch markets, use intelligence to evaluate opportunities, enforce your risk allowances, and manage trades automatically after you engage. Built-in numerical intelligence is included; additional AI services are optional.

The production experience we are building

  1. Install and open Nexus. Included components and sensible defaults reduce setup work.
  2. Securely connect and fund a trading account. The planned flow uses the selected financial provider’s secure authorization and funding pages, where bank linking is required. Bank funding is not implemented in this build; do not enter bank passwords or card details here.
  3. Select your risk allowance. Tell the engine what it may spend and which entry tiers it may use. Intelligence must stay within these limits.
  4. Engage. The goal is one clear action after required setup: automatic startup checks, then governed automated trading. Disengage pauses trading; Emergency Stop cuts execution independently.

Use the automated paper engine today

Keep the ready-made virtual budget and Terrestrial allowance, or change them in Risk. Choose Start Portal to run market observation and bounded learning, then Engage Paper. Nexus checks local setup, asks for confirmation and runs the fixed BTC paper experiment. Compatible unused virtual accounting is prepared automatically. No bank account, deposit or paid AI is needed for this local practice.

Today’s built-in council studies data and provides research; the fixed BTC policy makes simulated trading decisions. Learned council signals and optional AI do not yet direct orders. Validated intelligence-driven policies, connected execution, funding and computer-off operation remain release work. Existing choices and recovery blockers are preserved.

Beginner guide

Get started in a few steps

  1. To Do: read the automatically checked setup list. Optional connections can wait.
  2. Portal: Start Portal uses the ready-made BTC hourly research selections and starts bounded learning. Manual builds and different research settings are optional.
  3. Risk: new installations use $10,000 virtual cash and select Terrestrial. Keep these defaults or edit them; saved choices are preserved. Solar and Universal are optional.
  4. Accounts: Engage Paper creates compatible unused virtual accounting automatically. Separate manual initialization is optional; existing traded accounts require review. No money is deposited.
  5. Portal: Start Portal runs observation, bounded public history collection and built-in learning checks. Engage Paper separately starts the fixed BTC experiment with virtual funds. Council votes remain advisory.
  6. Stop and review: Stop Paper Session preserves holdings. Emergency Stop cuts paper execution and cancels outstanding simulated remainders while research continues. Reset never re-engages. Stop Portal turns the research engine off too; inspect recovery results.

Know what is running

Built-in AI: small trained numerical specialists work locally. They are not GPT-class language models. External AI is optional and separately billed when you explicitly send a request.

Account Matrix: Singularity shows total observed paper value; Fractal separates individual holdings. Gaps mean observations are missing.

Local simulation: no bank, payment method or broker is connected. Funding Preview is an illustration. Cloud operation is planned; Nexus currently needs this computer running.

Help on every control: hover or use keyboard focus for a practical explanation in the black-and-purple bottom-right panel. Move away or press Escape to dismiss it.

Keyboard tabs: Left/Right and Home/End move between workspace tabs. Your unsaved form entries remain while switching tabs. Hidden pages keep their existing session state.
