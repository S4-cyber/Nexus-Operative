Beginner guide

Get started in a few steps

  1. To Do: read the automatically checked setup list. Optional connections can wait.
  2. Research: select a market and history interval, then Build Hub & Learn. This downloads public prices and trains small built-in numerical models.
  3. Risk: save a virtual budget with allocations totaling 100%. Engage only a tier you intend to use for paper entries.
  4. Accounts: initialize virtual cash once if needed, then inspect or reconcile saved holdings. No money is deposited.
  5. Portal: Start Portal runs observation, bounded public history collection and built-in learning checks. Engage Paper separately starts the fixed BTC experiment with virtual funds. Council votes remain advisory.
  6. Stop and review: Stop Paper Session preserves holdings. Emergency Stop cuts paper execution and cancels outstanding simulated remainders while research continues. Reset never re-engages. Stop Portal turns the research engine off too; inspect recovery results.

Know what is running

Built-in AI: small trained numerical specialists work locally. They are not GPT-class language models. External AI is optional and separately billed when you explicitly send a request.

Account Matrix: Singularity shows total observed paper value; Fractal separates individual holdings. Gaps mean observations are missing.

Local simulation: no bank, payment method or broker is connected. Funding Preview is an illustration. Cloud operation is planned; Nexus currently needs this computer running.

Help on every control: hover or use keyboard focus for a practical explanation in the black-and-purple bottom-right panel. Move away or press Escape to dismiss it.

Keyboard tabs: Left/Right and Home/End move between workspace tabs. Your unsaved form entries remain while switching tabs. Hidden pages keep their existing session state.
